# Proposals

`learn` writes skill-candidate drafts here. They are **not** org skills.

A draft destined for neat-os or a stack pack is **project-agnostic** company guidance. Do not name a client, a product repository, a local path, or a ticket from the repo under audit. Project-specific facts belong in this repo's Agent Guide.

Elevation into neat-os (including `stacks/<name>/`) or a `neat-os-tool-*` leaf is a human PR. Do not auto-promote.

A draft stays here after it is elevated, and the playbook or principle links back to it. Drafts written before this tree was named neat-os are in the archive repository.
