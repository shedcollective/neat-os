# Kaizen

Goal: improve the OS from shipped work, in small steps.

Every repeated failure should change the system: a convention missed becomes a rule; missing project facts improve `AGENTS.md`; an unreliable sequence becomes a playbook; a mechanically detectable failure becomes a script. Keep extra machinery only while it earns its keep.
